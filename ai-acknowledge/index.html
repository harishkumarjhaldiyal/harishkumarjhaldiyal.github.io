Using AI in your learning: the guide, condensed

The full one-page draft is a separate document. This is the part students need to remember.

Green
Use it, and say so: explaining a concept you then read about yourself, brainstorming questions, checking your own understanding, feedback on the clarity of your own paragraph, learning a tool.
Amber
Ask your teacher first: translating a source, generating practice questions or sample data, summarising a source you have already read in full, any use in a task marked as independent.
Red
Never in assessed work: writing or rewriting your sentences, producing your analysis, code, calculations or conclusions, inventing or summarising sources you have not read, generating data, results, quotations or images as your own, entering personal information about anyone.

Acknowledge every use in one line where it helped. Work where it can be seen, in the school document system, so the history is your evidence. Privacy: approved tools, school accounts, no personal data, check the age limit. If something goes wrong: a conversation first, with the document history open; the Academic Honesty Policy applies where the guide has been broken.
