One gradebook for every maths summative

A department gradebook designed in Google Sheets. Teachers enter assessment-objective grades (Grades 9–10) or paper marks (IB Diploma); everything else — IB grade conversion from boundaries, class dashboards, supportive needs-attention flags, cross-class alignment for moderation and per-student profiles — updates automatically.
